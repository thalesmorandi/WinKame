# Code of conduct

WinKame follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and assume good intent.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who
repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer through [github.com/thalesmorandi](https://github.com/thalesmorandi).
Reports are handled privately.
